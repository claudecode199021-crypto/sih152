About the Project

Redefining Urban Living Through Integrated Design

UrbanNest is our response to SIH 2026 Problem Statement 26116 by Autodesk. We've designed a centrally located mixed-use building that seamlessly integrates commercial energy at the ground level with peaceful, nature-connected residential living above — all modeled in Autodesk Revit with full BIM precision.

Design Features

Architecture That Breathes & Inspires

Every element is purpose-driven — from climate-responsive facades to nature-integrated interiors that elevate both beauty and functionality.

Climate-Responsive Facade

Innovative shading elements, louvers, and perforated screens that respond to sunlight and heat while creating a stunning architectural expression.

Central Landscape Courtyard

The heart of the building — a lush green courtyard that brings natural light, ventilation, and a sense of calm to every floor it touches.

Structural BIM Excellence

Complete structural modeling with beams, columns, slabs, stairs, and reinforcement detailing — all built in Autodesk Revit with BIM precision.

Natural Ventilation Design

Cross-ventilation strategies, stack effect through the courtyard, and operable facades ensure fresh air circulation without heavy HVAC dependency.

EV-Ready Infrastructure

Basement parking with dedicated EV charging stations, smart energy management, and future-proof electrical infrastructure for sustainable mobility.

Community-First Spaces

Ground-floor retail hub, rooftop community terrace, co-working cafés, and shared green spaces that foster interaction and urban vibrancy.
